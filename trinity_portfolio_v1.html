About

Craft first.
Always.

I'm interested in what happens when editing, colour and sound work together to make a story feel something.

I'm Trinity John Oppermann — a video editor, colorist, and creative producer based in Bengaluru. With a BA in Visual Communication from St. Joseph's University and 2+ years of hands-on post-production, I've delivered 30+ projects spanning music videos, documentaries, brand campaigns, and social content.

What sets me apart is a rare overlap between visual editing and original music production. I've composed and scored complete soundtracks for short films in Logic Pro X — no libraries, no templates. I work the full pipeline: on-set camera and lighting, DaVinci Resolve colour grade, After Effects motion design, and final sound mix.

I approach every project like a cinematographer approaches a frame — every cut, colour choice, and sound decision has a reason.
